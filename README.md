## Please read before reviewing

- **All photography is stock placeholder. None of these are Total Build homes**, sites or
  employees. Real photography is still to come, and it will change how everything reads.
- **Two places deliberately show an empty state rather than a substitute:** the Schwartz residence
  on the Projects page, and Brian's portrait on About. Rather than put a stranger's house or face
  under a real name, those say plainly that the photography is still to come.
- **Copy is a working draft**, built from Brian's written interview answers of 3 August. It is
  accurate, and it is still being rewritten in Brian's and Mark's voice.
- **Nothing on the site states a fact we could not source.** Where a claim could not be verified it
  was left out rather than softened, which is why some pages are shorter than they will end up. No
  certifications appear anywhere, because none are confirmed. Same for any years-in-business number.
- **Direction B's copy is deliberately older** than the built site's. It is published to show the
  *layout*; its known-inaccurate claims have been corrected, but it has not had the full copy pass.
- The phone number is the one confirmed with Joe. The live site publishes a different one, an
  unresolved NAP conflict that is costing local search ranking and should be fixed before launch.
- Desktop is the priority. Mobile works and is solid, but is not finished.
