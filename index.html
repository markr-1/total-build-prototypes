Why work with us

The fourth generation of Smith builders.

Portrait of Brian Smith

The owner

We see ourselves as builders, coaches, and advocates.

Brian Smith grew up on his father’s job sites in Loudoun County. His grandfather ran E.J. Smith Builders in McLean, and his great-grandfather started the line in 1910.

After many successful years, his grandparents chose to close that company rather than risk anyone else carrying the family name without holding the standard behind it. Brian started Total Build in 2008, and he still runs it the way that decision taught him to.

Trust

We believe lasting relationships are built on honesty, transparency, and doing what’s right even when no one is looking.

Quality

There are materials we will not compromise on. We build the parts you never see to the standard of the parts you see every day.

Communication

You will know what is happening on your site, what it costs, and what comes next. Nothing arrives as a surprise at the end.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

01

We sit down

We start with your life in the home, how you actually want to live in it. What you want from the house shapes everything that follows.

02

We draw it

Plans and details, worked out together before a single board is cut. You see the whole house before anyone builds it.

03

We build it

A clean, careful site, and every step taken with your family in mind. We keep the work moving without letting the standard slip.

04

You take the keys

You walk into a house that is finished, and completely yours. Handing over the keys is the best day of the job.
