The projects

Two of the homes we’ve built.

Both are in Loudoun County. Past that, they have almost nothing in common.

01

Thornhedge Lane · Loudoun County

Timber, stone, and a long view

This house is built around one room. The stone fireplace runs the full height of it, with timber trusses overhead. Out back, a covered deck runs the width of the house and looks over open fields.

02

Sedgemont Ridge · Loudoun County

Built to face the view

Every room this family actually uses faces the same way. There is a deep covered porch, and a pool cut into the slope below it. The kitchen was built wide on purpose, because that is where people end up standing.

The process

How We Do It

01

We sit down

We start with your life in the home, how you actually want to live in it. Your hopes and dreams pave the way.

02

We draw it

Plans and details, worked out together before a single board is cut. Your vision is cast for review.

03

We build it

A clean, careful site. Each step taken with your family in mind. High speeds without neglecting excellence.

04

You take the keys

You walk into a home, completely yours and fully finished. Our greatest joy is placing the keys in your hands.
