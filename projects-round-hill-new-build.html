Round Hill, Virginia · New construction

A new custom home in Round Hill, Virginia

Built from the ground up in western Loudoun County.

The project

A second house in the same town.

Round Hill sits at the western edge of Loudoun County, where the lots get larger and the ground gets more opinionated. This one is new construction, a house built from bare dirt on the owners' own land.

It is the second home we have built in Round Hill. The other is a historic restoration a few minutes away, which is a fair illustration of what western Loudoun asks of a builder: some houses here are worth saving and some sites are worth starting fresh on, and knowing which is which is most of the job.

The restoration in Round Hill

The approach

Ground-up means the land decides first.

On a new build the early questions are not about the house at all. Where does the well go, and the septic. How does the site grade, and what does that do to the basement. Which way should the house face so that the rooms you live in get the light and the view. What has to go through the county before any of it can start.

Get those right and the house that follows is straightforward. Get them wrong and no amount of finish work rescues it.
