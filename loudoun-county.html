Loudoun County FAQ

What does a custom home cost in Loudoun County?

We would be guessing if we gave you a number before we walked your lot. Cost follows what your land requires, how large the house is, and the finishes you choose. We start from your non-negotiables and price the upgrades separately.

How long does Loudoun County take to approve a build?

We don't control the county, but what we can tell you is who to submit to, in what order, and what they'll ask for, because we've been through it here repeatedly. We set out the schedule for your specific home at the start and keep you posted on where it stands.

Do you build on my lot?

Yes, and we'll walk it with you before we draw anything. If you haven't bought land yet, we'll help you find it.

Is an older Loudoun property cheaper to build on?

It often is! Land with an older house on it may already have a driveway, a well, a septic system, or a usable foundation footprint. Putting all that in from scratch on a raw Loudoun lot eats a serious share of the budget.
