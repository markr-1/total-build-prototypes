Thornhedge Lane · Loudoun County, Virginia

The Thornhedge Lane house.

Timber, stone, and a room built to hold everybody.

The project

One room the whole house works around.

Some houses have a center and some don’t. This one does. A full-height stone fireplace sits under exposed timber trusses. The kitchen opens straight onto it, and the covered deck runs the length of the back, so on a good day the room just carries on outside.

Everything else is arranged around that. The kitchen is built for people to stand in rather than pass through. The entry is stone underfoot and timber overhead. The house tells you what it’s made of before you have your coat off.

The finished home

Inside.

The land

The lot decided most of this.

Open pasture, fence lines, and a long view. Where the house sits and which way it faces got settled before anything was drawn. On a property like this, those two decisions are worth more than any finish you can buy.
