A custom home on acreage in Leesburg, Virginia

Charmed living on more than three acres.

The project

Built for a family that entertains.

More than three acres in Leesburg, and a house designed to make use of them. Open from the front door through to the porch, so that the inside and the outside of the property are part of the same room when the weather allows it.

A lot of this size decides a great deal about the house that belongs on it. Where you put the home, which way it faces, what you see from the rooms you spend the most time in. That work happens before anything is drawn.

The approach

The land comes first.

We do not believe a house should simply be placed on a piece of land. It should be designed around the lot, the views, the landscape and the lifestyle the property makes possible. On acreage that matters more, not less. The same house sited two hundred feet away can be a completely different home to live in.

So we walk it first, and we talk about how the family actually lives before we talk about square footage.
