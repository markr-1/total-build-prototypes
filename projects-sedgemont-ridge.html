Sedgemont Ridge · Loudoun County, Virginia

The Sedgemont Ridge house.

Built to face the view, and to be lived in outside.

The project

Sited for the view, then built around it.

The rooms this family spends the most time in all face the same way. That sounds obvious. It isn’t, because it costs you something on the plan every time. It’s the difference between a house on a view and a house that uses one.

A deep covered porch runs along that side, wood ceiling over it. The pool is set into the slope below rather than parked on a flat pad. Inside, everything defers to the kitchen island, because that’s where people actually end up standing.

The finished home

Inside, and out.

The land

The slope was the opportunity.

Ground that falls away from the house is usually treated as a problem. Here it is the reason the pool sits where it does and the porch reads the way it does. Most of what makes this house what it is came from working with the grade instead of flattening it.
