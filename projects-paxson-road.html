Paxson Road · Loudoun County, Virginia

The Paxson Road house.

Timber, stone, and a room built to hold everybody.

The project

One room the whole house works around.

Some houses have a centre and some do not. This one does. A full-height stone fireplace sits under exposed timber trusses, the kitchen opens straight onto it, and the covered deck runs the length of the back so that on a good day the room simply carries on outside.

Everything else is arranged around that. The kitchen is built for people to stand in rather than pass through. The entry is stone underfoot and timber overhead, so the house tells you what it is made of before you have taken your coat off.

The finished home

Inside.

The land

The lot decided most of this.

Open pasture, fence lines, and a long view. Where the house sits and which way it faces were settled before anything was drawn, because on a property like this those two decisions are worth more than any finish you can buy.
