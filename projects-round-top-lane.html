Round Top Lane · Loudoun County, Virginia

The Round Top Lane house.

Built to face the view, and to be lived in outside.

The project

Sited for the view, then built around it.

The rooms this family spends the most time in all look the same way. That sounds obvious and it is not: it costs you something on the plan every time, and it is the difference between a house on a view and a house that uses one.

A deep covered porch runs along that side with a wood ceiling over it, and the pool is set into the slope below rather than parked on a flat pad. Inside, the kitchen island is the piece everything else defers to, because that is where people actually end up standing.

The finished home

Inside, and out.

The land

The slope was the opportunity.

Ground that falls away from the house is usually treated as a problem. Here it is the reason the pool sits where it does and the porch reads the way it does. Working with the grade rather than flattening it is most of what makes this house what it is.
