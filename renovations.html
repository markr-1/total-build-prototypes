Renovations

Whole-home renovations and additions in Loudoun County

Custom homes are most of what we do. We take on a small number of renovations alongside them, and we build them to the same standard.

Do you take on whole-home renovations?

Yes, selectively. We take on whole-home renovations, additions and historic work across Loudoun County and Northern Virginia, to the same standard as our custom homes. We're not a high-volume remodeling company, and we'll tell you straight if a project is better suited to someone else.

What we take on

Work that deserves the same care as a new build.

Whole-home renovations

Houses worth keeping, taken back far enough to do the job properly. What is behind the walls gets the same attention as what you see every day.

Additions and second stories

Adding to a house is harder than building one. The new work has to answer to what is already there, structurally and visually.

Historic work

Older homes in western Loudoun. Keep what gives the house its character, and rebuild everything else to a modern standard.

What we do not do

Fire, water and storm insurance restoration is a separate business, and not ours. If that's what you need, we'll point you to the right people.

The same standard as our custom builds

There is no second standard.

A renovation runs on the same principles as a custom home. Your non-negotiables first. Then a priced list of what else is possible, and honest numbers on what any change costs before you decide. There are materials we won't go below on a renovation either.

The two houses on our projects page are the clearest example of what that standard looks like.
