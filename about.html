About Total Build

There has been a Smith on a job site since 1910.

Total Build is a custom home builder in Leesburg, Virginia. Brian Smith is the fourth generation of his family to build houses. He didn't invent the standard he holds to. He inherited it from people who gave up a great deal to protect it.

The line

Four generations.

1910

Brian's great-grandfather starts contracting. He works on major concrete projects in Washington, D.C., including several national landmarks.

1950

His grandfather takes it up. He builds custom homes in McLean under his own name, E.J. Smith Builders.

1980

His father, Chris Smith, begins contracting in Loudoun County. He's still working today.

2000

Brian begins training under his father.

2008

Brian starts Total Build.

Where the standard came from

They closed a good company on purpose.

E.J. Smith Builders had a reputation in McLean for integrity and quality. When Brian's grandparents reached the end of their working life, they didn't sell the company. They didn't hand the name to anyone else to carry either.

They closed it. They weren't willing to risk the family name outliving the standard behind it.

Brian was young, and it stayed with him. It's why we're careful about what we put our name on, and why the answer to can you do it cheaper is sometimes no.

Brian Smith

The fourth Smith.

Some of Brian's earliest memories are of riding out to job sites with his father. Climbing the dirt piles from a newly excavated basement. The smell of fresh framing lumber and sawdust, and a house taking shape in front of him. He says walking onto one of his own sites still brings it back.

His father pushed him toward college so he wouldn't have to work as hard physically. Brian earned a degree in Communications from George Mason. Construction pulled him back anyway. What he worked out along the way was where he belonged in it: guiding projects, clients and teams, rather than working in the field.

He started Total Build in 2008. He sets the standard the company builds to. The day-to-day of your build belongs to your project manager.

Why custom homes

Every family is different, so every house is.

Brian expected this work to get repetitive. Someone hands you a set of plans and you build what's on the page. He was wrong, and it didn't take long to find out why.

A house comes down to more than square footage and finishes. It's the way one particular family lives in it. Where they gather. How they raise their children, and which room they all end up in at the end of the day. No two families answer that the same way, so no two houses we build come out the same.

Who you will actually work with

Builders, coaches, and advocates.

That's how Brian describes the job. No one person builds a custom home. You'll have a project manager who runs your site and answers your questions. Alongside them are superintendents, estimators, coordinators and the staff who keep a build moving.

The coaching part matters as much as the building. Most of our clients are doing this once. They arrive with an idea of the house and a hundred decisions they have never had to make, and our job is to see that every one of them gets made with the real numbers on the table. Then there's the advocating. We sit on your side of that table, including the parts of the job where it would be easier for us not to.

Technical skill is essential. So is character. If someone doesn't care about serving people, they're not a fit here, however good their hands are.

Here's where we differ from a lot of builders. Plenty of companies that advertise themselves as custom will quietly discourage changes once construction starts, because a change disrupts a production schedule. We don't. Changes affect cost and schedule, and we'll always tell you exactly how. But we'd rather adapt than hand you the house that was easiest for us to build.

What we hold to

The parts we don’t bend on.

Trust

We believe lasting relationships are built on honesty, transparency, and doing what’s right even when no one is looking.

Quality

There are materials we will not compromise on. We build the parts you never see to the standard of the parts you see every day.

Communication

You will always know where the job stands and what it is costing you. Nothing shows up as a surprise at the end.
