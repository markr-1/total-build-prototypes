The Team

A century-old standard.
A team that keeps it.

The Smith family has built to one standard since 1910. Brian started Total Build on it in 2008, and then he built a team that holds to it on every home.

Who builds your home

Builders, coaches, and advocates.

It takes a team to build a custom home. Your project manager runs the site and is the person you call. Around them are superintendents, estimators, coordinators, and the office staff who keep every build moving.

They coach you, too. Most families only build a custom home once. You come in with a picture of the house and a hundred decisions you’ve never had to make. Our team walks you through every one of them with the real numbers in front of you.

And they’re on your side. Want to change something once we’re building? They’ll tell you exactly what it does to cost and schedule, then help you decide. Plenty of builders who call themselves custom quietly talk people out of changes. We won’t.

We hire for character as much as for skill. Someone who doesn’t care about serving people won’t fit here, however good their hands are.

What every one of us holds to

The core of our standard.

These three decide who we hire, how we treat the families we build for, and which subcontractors we work with.

Trust

We believe lasting relationships are built on honesty, transparency, and doing what’s right even when no one is looking.

Quality

There are materials we will not compromise on. We build the parts you never see to the standard of the parts you see every day.

Communication

You will always know where the job stands and what it is costing you. Nothing shows up as a surprise at the end.

Where the standard came from

Four generations, since 1910.

1910

Brian’s great-grandfather starts contracting in Washington, D.C.

1950

His grandfather builds custom homes in McLean as E.J. Smith Builders.

1980

His father, Chris Smith, starts contracting in Loudoun County.

2008

Brian starts Total Build.

E.J. Smith Builders had a name in McLean for integrity. When Brian’s grandparents retired, they didn’t sell the company. They closed it, rather than let the family name outlast the standard behind it.

That standard is the one our team builds to today.

Founder and CEO

Brian Smith.

Brian grew up riding out to his father’s job sites, and he started Total Build in 2008. What he has built since is a team that holds his family’s standard without him in the room. We don’t take on more homes than our team can build to our standard.
