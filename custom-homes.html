How we build on your lot

Builders, coaches, and advocates.

That is how Brian describes the job, and it is the order the work actually happens in.

01

Before anything is drawn

We walk the land

A lot decides more about a house than most people expect. Grading, where the well and septic can go, setbacks, what the views actually are once the trees come down. If you have not bought land yet, that is common, and we can help you look.

02

Your goals, not the house

The non-negotiables

We work out the things that have to be right for this to be worth building at all. If you are spending the time, money and effort on a custom home, the finished house has to reflect what actually matters to your family.

03

A menu, with real numbers

The wish list, priced

Heated bathroom floors, under-cabinet lighting, a pot filler, upgraded trim, specialty windows. Most people have never been told what any of it costs, and you cannot prioritize what you cannot price.

04

Drawings, then the county

Design, engineering and permitting

This is the stretch most people underestimate, and the reason a genuinely custom home does not begin the week you sign. Much of it runs through Loudoun County, architects and engineers rather than through us, so we keep you current rather than quiet.

05

On site

The build, milestone by milestone

Your project manager runs the site and stays your point of contact. If you want to change something once we are underway, we will tell you exactly what it does to cost and schedule, and then help you decide rather than talk you out of it.

Where the money goes

We would rather you spend it well than spend it here.

We treat a client’s budget the way we would treat our own. There are unlimited ways to spend money on a custom home, and our job is to help you put it where your family will actually feel it.

Most people live in the kitchen, the great room and the primary suite, and that is usually where the money should go. Secondary bedrooms and bathrooms can be beautiful without consuming the budget. Luxury is not a matter of buying the most expensive product available. It is knowing where quality genuinely matters. There are materials we will not go below, because once you have seen the difference between an average product and an exceptional one it is hard to go back.

Before you call

The questions we get most.

How much does it cost to build a custom home in Northern Virginia?

There is no single number, and anyone who gives you one before seeing your lot is guessing. Cost follows three things: what your land requires, how large the house is, and the finishes you choose. We start from your non-negotiables, price the upgrades separately, and keep both in front of you, so the budget is something you steer rather than something you receive.

Is building a custom home cheaper than buying one?

Usually not. Building custom lets you get exactly what you want, and that level of personalization generally costs more than buying a house someone else already finished. What you are buying is a home designed around your lot, your views and the way your family actually lives, not a floor plan that happened to be for sale.

What happens before ground is broken?

Design, engineering, permitting and county approvals, in that order, and it is the stretch most people underestimate. Much of it runs through Loudoun County, architects, structural engineers and civil engineers, partners we work closely with but do not control. We set out the schedule for your specific home at the start and tell you where yours stands throughout.

Can I build for less on land that already has a house on it?

Often, yes. A property with an existing home may already have a driveway, a well, a septic system, or a foundation footprint you can reuse. That infrastructure is a real share of what it costs to build here. It is one of the more effective ways to make a custom home attainable, and it is worth checking before you rule a property out.
