How We Build.

Five stages, in the order they happen, and what you decide at each one.

01

Before anything is drawn

We walk the land

A lot decides more about a house than most people expect. Grading, where the well and septic can go, setbacks, what the views actually are once the trees come down. If you haven’t bought land yet, that’s common. We can help you look.

02

Your goals, not the house

The non-negotiables

We work out the things that have to be right for this to be worth building at all. You’re spending the time, the money and the effort on a custom home. The finished house has to reflect what actually matters to your family.

03

A menu, with real numbers

The wish list, priced

Heated bathroom floors, under-cabinet lighting, a pot filler, upgraded trim, specialty windows. Most people have never been told what any of it costs. You can’t prioritize what you can’t price.

04

Drawings, then the county

Design, engineering and permitting

This is the stretch most people underestimate. It’s also why a genuinely custom home doesn’t begin the week you sign. A lot of it runs through Loudoun County, the architects and the engineers, not through us. So we tell you where it stands instead of going quiet on you.

05

On site

The build, milestone by milestone

Your project manager runs the site and stays your point of contact. If you want to change something once we’re underway, we’ll tell you exactly what it does to cost and schedule. Then we help you decide, instead of talking you out of it.

Where the money goes

We’d rather you spend it well than spend it here.

We treat a client’s budget the way we’d treat our own. There are endless ways to spend money on a custom home. Our job is to help you put it where your family will actually feel it.

Most people live in the kitchen, the great room and the primary suite. That’s usually where the money should go. Secondary bedrooms and bathrooms can be beautiful without eating the budget. Luxury has less to do with buying the most expensive product available than with knowing where quality genuinely matters. There are materials we won’t go below. Once you’ve seen the difference between an average product and an exceptional one, it’s hard to go back.

Before you call

The Key Questions.

How much does it cost to build a custom home in Northern Virginia?

There’s no single number, and anyone who gives you one before seeing your lot is guessing. Cost follows what your land requires, how large the house is, and the finishes you choose. We start from your non-negotiables and price the upgrades separately. Both stay in front of you, so the budget is something you steer rather than something you receive.

Is building a custom home cheaper than buying one?

Usually not. Building custom lets you get exactly what you want, and that generally costs more than buying a house someone else already finished. What you get for it is a home designed around your lot, your views and the way your family actually lives, instead of a floor plan that happened to be for sale.

What happens before ground is broken?

Design, engineering, permitting and county approvals, in that order. It’s the stretch most people underestimate. A lot of it runs through Loudoun County, architects, structural engineers and civil engineers. We work closely with all of them and we don’t control any of them. We set out the schedule for your specific home at the start, and we tell you where yours stands the whole way through.

Can I build for less on land that already has a house on it?

Often, yes. A property with an existing home may already have a driveway, a well, a septic system, or a foundation footprint you can reuse. Putting that infrastructure in from scratch is a real share of what it costs to build here. It’s one of the better ways to make a custom home attainable, and it’s worth checking before you rule a property out.
